1. Scope and current service

CollabPro is a project that connects businesses and content creators to organize campaigns, applications, agreements, deliverables and results. These terms cover its presentation website and the use of the mobile application when the corresponding services are available.

The current website is informational. It does not create accounts, hire creators, process payments or activate subscriptions. Its contact form only performs local validation and simulates a response. No message is delivered and submitting it does not create a support request. Selecting a plan does not purchase it. Unavailable downloads and videos are not active services.
